About Me

I help professionals master English communication for the modern workplace. With a special focus on Business English and the IT sector, I bridge the gap between language learning and real-world professional success.

As an Online English Trainer and founder of Your English Hub, I design practical, conversation-based lessons tailored to each student’s unique goals and learning style. My approach goes beyond grammar; I focus on building the confidence and fluency needed to handle clients, lead meetings, and manage everyday workplace situations with clarity.

I hold a degree in Publishing from the University of Buenos Aires (UBA). This background in communication, combined with my passion for technology and digital tools, allows me to create innovative and interactive learning experiences.

Whether you are looking to boost your career in tech or improve your professional growth, I am here to help you reach your next milestone. Let’s start your English journey today!
